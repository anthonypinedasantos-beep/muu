Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains the small amount of cookie storage currently used by RBXCONDOS.

1. Cookie we use

We use one first-party cookie called rbx_cookie_choice. It stores your choice in the cookie notice so that the notice does not appear every time you visit.

  • Purpose: remember your cookie-notice choice.
  • Duration: up to 180 days.
  • Type: first-party preference cookie.

2. Analytics and advertising

RBXCONDOS does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics cookies.

3. Third-party services

Embedded or linked third-party services, including Discord and Roblox, may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with them. Their use is governed by their own policies.

4. Managing cookies

You can choose Accept or Reject in the website's cookie notice. You can also delete cookies through your browser settings. If you delete the preference cookie, the notice may appear again.

5. Contact

For questions, contact us through the RBXCONDOS Discord server.
