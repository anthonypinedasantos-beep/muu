Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how RBXCONDOS ("we", "our" or "the website") handles information when you visit rbxcondos.xyz or use the Condo Requestor.

1. Information we handle

RBXCONDOS does not ask you to create an account, provide your real name, or submit payment details. When you visit the website, the website host and service providers may process basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and security logs to deliver and protect the service.

The Condo Requestor contacts our game-discovery service to retrieve eligible Roblox experiences. The website does not intentionally ask you to submit personal information through this feature.

2. Third-party services

The website may request public community counts from Discord and link to Roblox and Discord. When you follow those links or your browser contacts those services, they may process information under their own privacy policies. We do not control their systems or practices.

3. Cookies and local preferences

We use a first-party cookie named rbx_cookie_choice to remember whether you accepted or rejected the cookie notice. It is stored for up to 180 days. We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.

4. How information is used

Technical information may be used to operate the website, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain security. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data retention and security

Technical logs may be retained by hosting or infrastructure providers according to their own retention and security practices. We take reasonable steps to keep the website secure, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your privacy rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may have rights under applicable data-protection law, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority. The availability of each right depends on the circumstances and applicable law.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the RBXCONDOS team through our official Discord server.
