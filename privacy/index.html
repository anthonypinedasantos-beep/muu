Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how RBXCONDOS ("we", "our" or "the website") handles information when you visit rbxcondos.xyz or use the Condo Requestor.

Adults only — 18+. RBXCONDOS is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 or older. The website is not directed at children or anyone under 18. If you are under 18, do not use this website. We do not currently operate an age-verification system, so this notice is an eligibility requirement and not proof of a visitor's age.

RBXCONDOS does not ask you to create an account, provide your real name, or submit payment details. When you visit the website, the website host and service providers may process basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and security logs to deliver and protect the service.

The Condo Requestor contacts our game-discovery service to retrieve Roblox experiences. The website does not intentionally ask you to submit personal information through this feature.

The website may request public community counts from Discord and link to Roblox and Discord. When you follow those links or your browser contacts those services, they may process information under their own privacy policies. We do not control their systems or practices.

RBXCONDOS also uses third-party advertising scripts from Monetag and Quge5 Multitag. They are loaded only after you accept the cookie notice. Once loaded, the providers may process technical data such as your IP address, browser/device information, identifiers and advertising interactions, and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies.

3. Cookies and local preferences

We use a first-party cookie named rbx_cookie_choice to remember whether you accepted or rejected the cookie notice. It is stored for up to 180 days. If you accept, third-party advertising scripts may set or access cookies and similar technologies as described in the Cookie Policy. If you reject, the advertising scripts are not loaded.

Technical information may be used to operate the website, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain security. We do not sell personal information.

Technical logs may be retained by hosting or infrastructure providers according to their own retention and security practices. We take reasonable steps to keep the website secure, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.
